
- **AI train on content without permission**
  - **Theft argument**: Creators worked hard, no payment → unfair, AI copies style → feels wrong (Value: **fairness**)
  - **Permitted use argument**: Humans also learn from others, AI does the same, it doesn’t copy exactly (Value: **accessibility**)
  - **Key conflict**: Values: Fairness vs Accessibility vs Equality (power to big companies)
  - **Analysis**: Ultilitarian: good (more access, innovation), bad (unfair to creators). Deontology: unfair (no permission, no payment), but also not stealing (AI is different from copying).


- **Discrimination** = using irrelevant data to judge people like gender, ethnicity, religion
  - **Amazon hiring** (preferred men → trained on past data → bias copied), **Coded Bias Face recognition** (white bias → skewed data), **COMPAS** (crime risk → biased + not transparent), **Toeslagenaffaire** (used nationality → labeled people as fraud risk)
  - **Duty ethics**: unfair like: Two equal people → treated differently → morally wrong
  - **Utilitarian**: creates inequality, reduces trust, harms society

**Sources of bias**:
  - **Skewed data**: some groups underrepresented → AI learns wrong patterns
  - **Historical bias**: past discrimination → copied by AI
  - **Hidden variables**: AI finds patterns we don’t see → can infer sensitive info (gender)

- **Transparency problem**
  - **black box** → Decisions not explainable
  - **No defense** → cannot argue against decision
- **Solution**: FAT (Fair, Accountable, Transparent)
  - **Fair**: treat people equally
  - **Accountable**: someone responsible for decisions not algorithm (DS, company, government)
  - **Transparent**: explain how decisions made
- **Data Types**:
  - **Fair data** allowed: based on your actions (work experience, financial history)
  - **Unfair data** not allowed: irrelevant + sensitive (gender, ethnicity, religion)
  - **Doubtful data** tricky: not chosen by you (surname, zip code (Ok: insurance and risking area, Not Ok: loan decision))

- **Accuracy vs fairness**
  - **Acurate AI** (Utilitarian): + Better predictions, - no explanation
  - **Explainable AI**: + fairer (Deontology), - less accurate

- **Generative AI & bias**: Should AI reflect reality or improve it?
  - **Reality**: truthful but may reinforce stereotypes
  - **Ideal world**: fair but unrealistic
  - No perfect answer
  
- **Sustainability** (energy use): AI uses energy → environmental impact
- **Criti-hype**: people exaggerate problems → AI energy use is not the biggest issue, sometimes humans use more
  - **Utilitarian**: bad (environmental harm) future generations matters, but also good (AI can help solve problems)
  - **Deontology**: duty to protect planet, but also duty to innovate and improve
