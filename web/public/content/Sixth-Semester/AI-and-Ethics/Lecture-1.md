Ethics = the study of what is right and wrong.

- Ethics vs Law:
    - Something can be legal but unethical (example: paying low wages)
    - Something can be illegal but ethically justified (e.g. stealing to feed a starving child)

- **Ethics and AI relation**:
    - **Ethical AI systems**: How to design AI that behaves correctly. (Self-driving cars, Military drones, Medical AI)
    - **Ethical behavior of developers**: How you behave as an AI student or engineer. (Data misuse, Biased datasets, Using AI dishonestly)
    - **Ethical impact of AI on society**: How AI affects society. (AI increasing inequality, AI replacing jobs, Deepfakes, Surveillance)

- Ethics = Value Judgements (Try to balance values like safety, freedom, fairness, dignity, trust)
- Value judgements come with emotions, but that doesn't mean they are irrational.
    - Emotions often reflect values

- **Ethical Theories**
    - **Utilitarianism** (Consequence Ethics): The best action is the one that produces the most happiness and the least suffering.
        - **Utilitarian question**: Positive side questions $\to$ if yes → justified
        - **Strengths**: Matches intuition, flexible, easy to understand
        - **Weaknesses**: Hard to measure happiness, can justify strange things, no clear moral rules
        - **Problem example**: Sacrificing one innocent person to save five people. Utilitarianism $\to$ acceptable $\to$ many feel it's wrong.
    - **Deontology** (Duty Ethics): Some actions are right or wrong regardless of consequences. Focus on rules and principles.
        - **Deontology question**: Does it violate a moral rule(Dont lie,harm , keep promises, treat well, privacy)? If yes → wrong
        - **Strengths**: Matches intuition, clear rules, predictable
        - **Weaknesses**: Where do rules come from? Hard to handle exceptions
        - **Problem example**: Should you lie to save someone? Deontology : no lying
    - **Virtue Ethics**: Focus on the character: a good person does the right thing. Focuses on **moral character** instead rules or calculations
        - **Virtues**: Courage, honesty, generosity, friendliness, modesty, temperance
        - **Virtue ethics question**: What would a good person do?
        - **Strengths**: Very human, reflects real moral life
        - **Weaknesses**: Circular definition, hard to apply, which virtues matter?
