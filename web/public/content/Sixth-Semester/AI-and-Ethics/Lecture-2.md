- **Privacy**: The right to be left alone, No arbitrary interference with your private life, home, or correspondence.
    - **Human rights**: Privacy is a human right, but not an absolute one. It can be violated if justified (e.g. for safety).
    - **Tatto example**: observer also “owns” what they see
- **Privacy & Online Ads Dilemma**: Better service vs loss of privacy
    - **Arguments AGAINST (mostly duty ethics)**
        - Data is private (messages = correspondence)
        - Feels wrong → violation of rights
        - Companies know too much
        - Anonymization doesn’t really work
    - **Arguments FOR (often utilitarian)**
        - Free services
        - Better recommendations
        - “You agreed to terms”
        - Trade-off: data ↔ service
    - Utilitarianism allows more
    - Deontology is stricter

- **Targeted advertising ethical?**
    - **Utilitarian**: Good service, no cost → acceptable
    - **Deontology**: Uses private data → violation of rights

- **CCTV**: Main trade-off **Privacy vs Safety**
    - **Utilitarian arguments AGAINST**: Not effective, Unintended consequences (abuse, hacking), Slippery slope (towards control society)
    - **Duty-based arguments**: Feels wrong to be constantly watched, Loss of freedom, Asymmetry (you don’t see who watches you)
    - **Asymmetry**:
        - Police officer: You see them → fair
        - CCTV, You are watched, but don’t see watcher → unfair

---

- **Deepfakes**: AI-generated fake videos/audio that look real.
    - **Utilitarian**: Fraud (25M case), Misinformation, Scams
    - **Truth loses value**
    - **Identity theft**: stealing someone’s face/voice, forging identity
    - **Lack of consent**: People are used without permission
    - **Ethical analysis**:
        - **Utilitarian**: Bad → scams, harm, misinformation - Sometimes okay → jokes, movies
        - **Deontology**: Lying is wrong, Using someone’s identity without consent → wrong
        - **Virtue ethics**: Depends on intention (Good: harmless joke , Bad: greed, manipulation, humiliation)

- **Denmark solution**: Created **Portrait rights** meaning: You cannot use someone’s face/voice without permission
    - Rights can be invented (Like copyright)
